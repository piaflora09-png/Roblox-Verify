Discord & Roblox Account Verification

Connect your Roblox account securely to your Discord server profile using Roblox OAuth 2.0. Your password and session cookies are never accessed.

  1. Go to your Discord server and run /verify.
  2. Click Authorize on Roblox on the bot's message.
  3. Sign in to Roblox and approve the requested scopes.
  4. Return to Discord to see your verified role and nickname update.
